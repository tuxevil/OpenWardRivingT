# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in OpenWardRivingT a welcoming, harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity,
sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

This project is **active** wireless auditing software. The maintainers
recognise a special responsibility to refuse assistance with, and to actively
discourage, the use of this project for unauthorised attacks on networks the
operator does not own. Conduct that conflicts with that responsibility — for
example, publishing tutorials that target third-party networks, sharing
captured hashes from networks without written authorisation, or encouraging
others to do so — is itself a violation of this code of conduct.

## Expected behaviour

- Be welcoming. Assume good faith. Ask for clarification rather than assuming
  malice.
- Be respectful. Disagree on the technical merits; never on the person.
- Focus on what is best for the community and the wireless security community
  at large.
- Show empathy towards other community members.
- When in doubt, defer to the [README → Legal & ethics](README.md#legal--ethics)
  section. If you are unsure whether a use case is authorised, **stop and
  ask the network owner in writing first.**

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination in any form.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political
  attacks.
- Publishing or soliciting captured material from networks the operator does
  not own or have explicit written authorisation to test.
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or electronic
  address, without their explicit permission.
- Sharing or encouraging the use of project binaries against systems the
  operator does not own.
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Enforcement responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing the standards
of acceptable behaviour and will take appropriate and fair corrective action
in response to any behaviour that they deem inappropriate, threatening,
offensive, or harmful.

Maintainers have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject
comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and other contributions that are
not aligned with this code of conduct, and to ban temporarily or permanently
any contributor for behaviours that they deem inappropriate, threatening,
offensive, or harmful.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies within all project spaces — the GitHub
repository, issue tracker, discussions, pull request reviews, and any other
channel the project operates — and also applies when an individual is
representing the project in public spaces (for example, using an official
project email address, posting via an official social media account, or
acting as an appointed representative at an event).

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behaviour may be
reported to the maintainers via the private contact listed in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md#reporting-a-vulnerability) (use the same channel
and mark the subject as `CoC report`). All complaints will be reviewed and
investigated promptly and fairly.

All maintainers are obligated to respect the privacy and security of the
reporter of any incident.
